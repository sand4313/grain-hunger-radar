        How the score works

        Each country gets a risk score from 0 to 100, built from four parts. Parts without data are left out and the rest re-weighted, so the coverage figure next to each score shows how many of the four were available.

        • Hunger, 35%: share of people in IPC Phase 3 or worse. 40% scores the maximum.
        • Currency, 20%: loss against the dollar over 30 days. A 10% fall scores the maximum.
        • Wheat stocks, 25%: ending stocks as a share of a year's use. Below 0.10 scores the maximum, above 0.40 scores zero.
        • Import dependence, 20%: imports as a share of use.

        Stocks and imports count for less in richer countries, because a wealthy importer with thin stocks buys more instead of going hungry: high income counts 30%, upper-middle 70%.

        The tender signal is separate: high when a country imports at least 40% of its wheat and holds 0.20 or less of a year's use in stock. It is a heuristic, not a forecast.
